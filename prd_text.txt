1.1 Product Overview  
REGIQ (Regression Intelligence Quotient) is an enterprise -grade, AI -powered Software -as-a-Service 
(SaaS) platform purpose -built for Fortune 500 organizations operating on ServiceNow ITSM. It unifies 
intelligent regression test suite management, automated i mpact analysis, defect intelligence, and 
predictive release quality scoring into a single cohesive platform.  
REGIQ eliminates the chronic inefficiency of manual regression suite maintenance by applying advanced 
machine learning, natural language processing, graph -based dependency analysis, and AI -assisted 
impact recommendations. The platform continuously ingests ServiceNow metadata —update sets, 
business rules, workflows, API changes, catalog items, and integrations —and translates this change 
intelligence into actionable, risk -ranked regression test suites in minutes rather than days.  
1.2 Enterprise QA Challenges Addressed  
Enterprise ServiceNow environments routinely host 10,000 –50,000+ test cases spanning dozens of 
ITSM modules, hundreds of third -party integrations, and complex multi -process workflows. The absence 
of intelligent tooling creates cascading problems:  
• Regression suites are built manually by tribal knowledge, leading to coverage gaps and over -
testing  
• Impact analysis requires days of cross -functional meetings with no data -driven foundation  
• Defect leakage to production causes SLA breaches and customer trust erosion  
• Release cycles extend by 30 –60% due to undifferentiated full -suite regression runs  
• Teams lack visibility into upstream/downstream test case dependencies  
1.3 AI -Powered Testing Vision  
REGIQ's AI vision is predicated on four pillars: Intelligent Classification (every test case is automatically 
classified by business process, type, criticality, and dependency), Autonomous Impact Detection (change 
events trigger real -time identification of  impacted test cases with confidence scores), Predictive Defect 
Intelligence (historical defect patterns power proactive risk identification before releases), and Continuous 
Learning (every test execution, defect, and release outcome feeds back into the AI  models to improve 
accuracy over time).

2.1 Complexity of Enterprise ServiceNow Test Repositories  
Large enterprises deploying ServiceNow ITSM accumulate test repositories that routinely exceed 
15,000 –50,000 individual test cases. These repositories span core ITSM modules (Incident, Change, 
Problem, CMDB), service management extensions (HRSD, ITOM, SecO ps), and complex third -party 
integrations (SAP, Workday, Microsoft Azure DevOps, Jira, PagerDuty). Managing this corpus manually 
is untenable at enterprise scale.  
2.2 Manual Regression Suite Maintenance  
Without intelligent tooling, regression suite maintenance relies entirely on individual QA engineer 
expertise. Suites become stale between releases, failing to reflect architectural changes, new 
integrations, or deprecated functionality. Maintenance overhe ad consumes 20 –30% of total QA 
bandwidth in large organizations.  
2.3 Difficulty Identifying Impacted Test Areas  
When a ServiceNow update set modifies business rules, workflow activities, or API schemas, identifying 
the full blast radius of impacted test cases requires manual tracing across dependency chains. Without 
automated dependency graphs, critical regression g aps are frequently missed, leading to defect escapes 
that manifest in production.  
2.4 High Testing Effort and Slow Release Cycles  
The absence of intelligent test selection forces teams to run full regression suites for every release. This 
non-selective approach inflates testing cycles by 3 –5x compared to optimized impact -based regression. 
Large enterprises report 4 –8 week testing win dows that delay business -critical deployments and 
accumulate technical debt.  
2.5 Defect Leakage to Production  
Industry benchmarks indicate that 15 –25% of production defects in ServiceNow environments could have 
been detected through adequately scoped regression testing. Root causes include: unidentified 
dependency chains in impact analysis, test case mapping gaps in modified workflow areas, inadequate 
coverage of third -party integration regression points, and failure to re -test historically unstable modules.  
2.6 Lack of Intelligent Prioritization and Cross -Module Dependency 
Complexity  
ServiceNow's architecture creates deep cross -module dependencies. A single business rule modification 
can cascade through Incident routing logic, Change risk assessment, CMDB relationship queries, and 
Service Catalog fulfillment workflows simultaneously. W ithout a dependency graph engine, QA teams 
have no systematic method to identify and validate this blast radius.

© 2025 REGIQ Platform Division. All rights reserved.  Page 8 of 46 3. Product Vision  
REGIQ envisions a future state where enterprise QA operations in ServiceNow environments are 
predominantly autonomous. The platform's north star is zero -effort regression planning: where change 
detection, dependency analysis, test selection, suite optimiza tion, and execution scheduling occur with 
minimal human intervention, guided by transparent AI recommendations that QA engineers trust and 
validate.  
3.1 Autonomous Intelligent QA Operations  
The platform progressively automates QA decision -making from descriptive (what changed) to diagnostic 
(what is impacted) to prescriptive (what should be tested) to predictive (what is likely to fail). This maturity 
progression aligns with enterprise AI ado ption curves and builds stakeholder confidence at each stage.  
3.2 AI -Driven Impact Analysis  
At the core of the vision is an Impact Analysis Engine that operates in real time as update sets are 
promoted, requirements are raised, or configuration changes are committed. The engine combines graph 
traversal across dependency trees, vector similarity m atching across test case embeddings, historical 
failure probability scoring, and business criticality weights to produce a ranked, confidence -scored list of 
test cases that must be included in the regression suite for a given change.  
3.3 Smart Regression Optimization  
REGIQ's Regression Suite Optimizer eliminates redundancy, detects overlapping coverage, balances 
execution time against risk coverage, and generates multiple execution strategies (Fast Track, Standard, 
Full Coverage) enabling release managers to make infor med time -vs-risk trade -offs backed by data.  
3.4 Predictive Release Quality Management  
Beyond reactive testing, REGIQ provides predictive release quality scoring: a composite metric that 
factors historical defect density per module, change complexity score, coverage completeness, and team 
execution velocity to forecast the probability of pro duction incidents before release sign -off.

11.3 Impact Traversal Algorithms  
11.3.1 Breadth -First Traversal for Downstream Impact  
When a component is modified (e.g., a Script Include), REGIQ performs a breadth -first traversal starting 
from the modified node and following outbound DEPENDS_ON and TRIGGERS relationships to identify 
all potentially impacted downstream components. The tra versal applies depth -based weight decay to 
modulate confidence scores.  
The traversal uses a maximum depth of 4 hops as default (configurable per tenant) to prevent unbounded 
graph walks in highly interconnected ServiceNow instances. A visited -node set prevents re -traversal of 
already -visited nodes, ensuring O(V+E) complexity.  
11.3.2 Upstream Dependency Tracing  
For understanding what dependencies a changed component relies upon (upstream analysis), the DME 
performs a reverse traversal following inbound edges. This identifies components that the modified 
element depends on, which may need verification testing even  if not directly changed.  
11.3.3 Critical Path Analysis  
For business -critical change sets, REGIQ computes critical paths through the dependency graph using 
a weighted shortest -path algorithm. Paths through high -criticality business processes receive elevated 
risk scores, ensuring that test cases covering critic al paths are always included in regression suites

rate-limited per tenant and per user with standard HTTP 429 responses.
